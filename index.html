About me

I'm Aubrey, but call me Aubs. I got bored, and I'm friends with Digital Multimedia Design students. It started in June, before our winter term. They had to buy a camera for their class, so I joined in the fun too, even though my discipline is Accountancy.

I mainly shoot for clubs right now to build up my portfolio, and I plan to hone my skills further.
