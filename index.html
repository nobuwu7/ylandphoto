About me

I'm Aubrey, but call me Aubs. I'm Sarawakian, and I study Accountancy at Swinburne University of Technology, Sarawak Campus. I shoot photo and video for clubs and events.

It started in June, before our winter term. I got bored, and my friends in Digital Multimedia Design had to buy a camera for class, so I joined in the fun too. Then I looked at a frame and thought, "Oh, I took that." I've been hooked since.

At events with speakers, the speaker's point of view often goes unseen, so I make sure I cover them and everybody else in the room. My favourite frames are the genuine ones, so I'll make you laugh to get that joy on camera.

My accounting side shows up in my planning. My shot lists are meticulous, and I like to say I audit my own work. On the day, I keep the shoot free flow so the plan never boxes in the creativity.

Right now I shoot for several clubs while I build my portfolio, and I'm always working to sharpen my skills.
